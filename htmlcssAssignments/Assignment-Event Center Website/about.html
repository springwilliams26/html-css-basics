Our Story

Opened in 2024, The Dip was opened with one goal in mind: to bring unforgettable entertainment to Palm Harbor. With state of the art sound, immersive lighting, and space for 600 guests, we've hosted entertainers from across the country, with special appearances by our local legends.

Our Mission

  • Support emerging artists and showcase global talent
  • Provide a safe, inclusive space for all music lovers
  • Deliver world-class sound and unforgettable experiences
